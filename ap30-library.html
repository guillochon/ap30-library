AP30 Library

Manage the music on a Hidizs AP30's SD card. Covers are cleaned up as music goes in.

This page needs Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Other browsers can't read and write folders on your drives.

Getting started: plug the AP30 in (or put its microSD card in a card reader), click Open SD card… and choose the card's drive, for example F:. Chrome will ask to allow editing; say yes.

If Chrome won't open the drive itself, make a folder such as Music on the card and choose that folder instead. Everything happens on your computer; nothing is uploaded.

SD card format

Browsers can't format drives, so formatting is done with a separate tool. Formatting erases everything on the card. Copy back your music with Add music… afterwards.

Windows: Windows only makes FAT32 on cards up to 32 GB. For bigger cards use Ridgecrop's free fat32format in an administrator terminal, with the card's drive letter:

fat32format -c64 F:

macOS: find the card's disk number with diskutil list, then:

diskutil eraseDisk FAT32 AP30 MBRFormat /dev/diskN

Linux: with the card's partition unmounted:

sudo mkfs.vfat -F 32 -s 64 -n AP30 /dev/sdX1

The format is detected from how the card stores file times (FAT32 rounds them to 2 seconds, exFAT to 10 ms), so the page can tell FAT32 from exFAT but not the cluster size.
